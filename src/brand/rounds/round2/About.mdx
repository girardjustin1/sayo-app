# Round 2: Ink, Clay, Quiet, Grove (current)

**What we tried.** The pink logo stays, but the rest of the system moves off pink. Guided by
[AskTia](https://asktia.com): cream and sand grounds, coral only on the main action, a light editorial serif with
one italic accent word, line icons, restrained shapes.

| Direction | In one line | Tagline |
|---|---|---|
| **D · Ink** | Deep ink navy and crisp paper; private and premium | “Ask anything. Privately.” |
| **E · Clay** | Cream, sand, terracotta, wine and marigold; warm and grounded (default) | “Your health, in your own words.” |
| **F · Quiet** | Linen and charcoal; the logo is almost the only colour; maximum discretion | “Say it here. It stays here.” |
| **G · Grove** | White pages, deep forest hero panels, lime buttons, two-tone headlines; built on [Hers](https://www.forhers.com) | “Your questions deserve real answers.” |

**Why.** Round 1 was pink everywhere: it read as “women’s health” from across a room (bad on a shared phone), sat in
the FemTech default, and hid the logo. See *Round 1 (pink) → About this round*.

**Two references.** D, E and F follow AskTia: calm grounds, coral only on the main action, a light serif. G follows
Hers: one tight sans, two-tone headlines, bento tiles, guided entry (“Not sure where to begin? Start here”), pill
buttons. See *Research → 08 Hers (for Grove)* for what SAYO takes and what it leaves (sales, prices, outcome claims).

**In this round.**

- **Directions & tokens:** the four directions side by side, their voice and copy, and live Astryx tokens.
- **Imagery:** the same ten image slots per direction (splash hero, wide banner, four pillar tiles, three Learn
  topics, empty state), generated with Higgsfield to each direction’s brief.
- **Landing page:** a full marketing page per direction, so each brand can be judged as a whole.
- **In the app:** every user screen was rebuilt Astryx-first with iOS control sizes; new Hers-style screens
  (bento Home, guided start, Learn) live under *User*. The robot icon was replaced by the SAYO o-dot AI mark.
