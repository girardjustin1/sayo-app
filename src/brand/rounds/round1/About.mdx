# Round 1: the pink set

**What we tried.** Three personalities, each taking its main colour from inside the logo:

| Direction | In one line | Tagline |
|---|---|---|
| **A · Big Sister** | Warm, close, a little playful; rose and peach | “Say it. We’ve got you.” |
| **B · Clear Care** | Calm, trustworthy, discreet; plum and lavender | “Private questions. Trusted answers.” |
| **C · Unmuted** | Bold, stigma-breaking; magenta gradient, Fraunces serif | “No shame. Just say it.” |

**Imagery in this round.**

1. **Imagery 1 · First pass:** one Higgsfield set per direction (10 images).
2. **Imagery 2 · Six approaches:** candid, faceless, editorial, illustrated, soft 3D objects, brand-built
   abstraction, each art-directed for A, B and C (48 images). All six are kept for now.

**What we learned.** All three read as pink: they differed in temperature, not colour family. Astryx tinted every
grey toward the accent, so even neutrals turned blush or lilac. Pink is the category default and makes the app
recognisable as “women’s health” on a shared phone. That led to round 2.
