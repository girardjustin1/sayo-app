# SAYO: Storybook drafts

<Hero />

SAYO gives young women a **private, stigma-free place to ask sensitive health questions**, understand what they may
need, get verified professional guidance where appropriate, and find the right care. This Storybook is **phase 1**:
every screen, state and brand idea as a story, before any backend exists. Nothing here talks to a server, and every
person, clinic and case is **fictional sample data**.

**Why it exists:** to agree on the product (the flows, the safety rules, the words and the look) while changing them
is still a quick edit, and to give the founder something real to test with pilot users and show to funders.

## The bridge SAYO builds

The whole MVP proves one thing: SAYO can safely move someone from silence to the right care.

## Rules every screen follows

- **AI never diagnoses.** Every AI answer is labelled AI and carries the locked line *“This is general health information, not a diagnosis.”*
- **AI, peers and professionals never blur.** Only Verified Professionals get the green ✓ badge.
- **Privacy first.** 4-digit PIN, nickname and generated avatar in the Circle, private Ask SAYO history never visible there.
- **Discreet notifications.** Lock-screen text never names a health topic.
- **Safety beats the questionnaire.** In GBV and red-flag moments, urgent information comes first, and **Quick exit** is always one tap away.
- **Locked copy.** Safety and clinical wording is the same in every direction (see <A href="./?path=/story/brand-round-2-current-directions-tokens--voice-and-copy">Voice & Copy</A>).

## How to look around

- **Toolbar → Direction** switches every screen between D · Ink, E · Clay, F · Quiet and G · Grove (and the round-1 pink set).
- **User/** is the mobile app (390 × 844 phone). **Professional/** and **Admin/** are desktop web tools.
- Every important state has its own story: empty, waiting, error, red flag, offline, urgent…
- **Brand/** holds the shared logo and one folder per round of exploration (directions, voice and copy, live Astryx tokens, imagery). Round 2 is current; round 1 (pink) is kept for comparison.
- **Planning/** holds the tech decisions, open questions and next steps.

## Built on Astryx

Screens are composed from [Astryx](https://astryx.atmeta.com), Meta’s open-source design system (React 19, 150+
accessible components, themed with CSS custom properties). Each brand direction is an Astryx theme, so choosing a
direction later is a one-line change, and the professional and admin tools get tables, navigation and forms for free.
