## Guardrails
- SAYO sells nothing. No prices, plans, statistics or outcome promises.
- No real person or clinic. No medication brand names.
- Locked safety/clinical copy stays word for word (`LOCKED_COPY`).
- This is inspiration, not imitation: never use Hers's logo, name, photos or copy.

## Corrections after the deeper study (see `hers.md`)
- Lime is **not** the general primary button. On the site it appears only on dark forest panels; on white the main button is a dark pill. Grove now does the same.
- Quizzes are one question per screen but often multi-select with a Continue button, not always single-tap.
- Hers's own message threads use initials, a credential badge and a licence number for clinicians, so SAYO's initials-only professionals match the reference.
- Hers is **not** a privacy reference: "discreet" there means shipping, and the app asks for legal name, date of birth and phone. SAYO's privacy rules stay stricter.
