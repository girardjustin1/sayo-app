# The Circle: pseudonymous peer community

Mobbin research for SAYO, 2026-11-10. "Image" links expire around 2026-12-10. All screens are iOS unless marked.

SAYO's rules for the Circle: usernames and avatars only, no photos, no DMs; posts, replies, reactions and reporting; Verified Professional replies look different from peer replies; the Circle identity is kept separate from private health history.

- **Quizlet: a "Verified" chip beside the heading**: [screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/0205337f-dbee-4a2e-aa43-43668ad2cdb7)

**Combined takeaway:** a Verified Professional reply needs three things at once:
1. a tick plus the words "Verified Professional", so colour is not the only signal,
2. a role line (e.g. "Nurse, Accra"), and
3. a tinted card background, so it stands out even when scrolling fast.

Peer replies keep the neutral style.

## Report flow

- **Reddit: "Submit a Report"**: [screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/4de80dc1-c1a3-49f6-ba58-14390c77e091) · [image](https://mobbin.com/api/mcp/short/O5sCdy9F)
  - Reason chips include Harassment, Threatening violence, Sharing personal information, Non-consensual intimate media, Self-harm or suicide, Impersonation and Spam.
  - Tapping a chip shows its definition below.
  - A second step narrows the reason ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/d6c26f82-0246-4105-b1f2-449b443d2241)), including "Unsolicited chat or private messaging".

  **Takeaway:** the best taxonomy for SAYO. Add "Wrong or harmful health information" and "Someone may be in danger" as SAYO-specific reasons. The second one should route to the safety team, not only to moderation.
- **Character AI: report flow**: [flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/4718a748-8105-4e58-868f-f92a90cfbcad)
  A sheet offers "Hide from feed / Report", then a reason category, then sub-reason. **The post is immediately replaced** with "Thanks for reporting this post. Your feedback is important in keeping our community safe." plus "Show post / Hide from feed". Dark UI.
  **Takeaway:** hide reported content from the reporter straight away and thank them.
- **QUITTR: report flow**: [flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/d223796e-9bee-483a-bcf4-cdc6a73f30bf)
  A "…" menu offers "Report Post / Block User", then a toast says "Post has been reported." The post stays visible.
  **Weaker:** no reason is captured and the content stays on screen.
- **corner: long-press shows a single "report" pill with no visible confirmation**: [flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/a3414b3e-d8ed-4391-a364-796e2c63ced1)
  **Weak**, because users don't know whether anything happened.
- **YouTube (web): report history**: [screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/8cb052c0-2040-4429-a33d-c39cbf8de2b7) · [image](https://mobbin.com/api/mcp/short/cjnKCEMw)
  "Thanks for reporting" plus a table of your reports with their status.
  **Takeaway (later):** close the loop by telling reporters the outcome.
